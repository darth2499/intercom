Tips

  • Tap Rewind or Forward to skip, hold to scrub.
  • Faders jump to your finger; lit S/M/R buttons show the MixPre's state.
  • Keyboard mode can't read the MixPre back, so S/M/R lights are a guess and faders need MIDI.
  • Assign U1–U5 on the MixPre: Menu › ★ Shortcuts › Control Surface.
