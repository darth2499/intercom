Simple copies the recorder's own screen (MixPre-6 II or 10 II). Advanced is the full remote.

MixPre model

Sets the channels and the Simple screen. Auto follows the MixPre linked over Bluetooth; pick one by hand for when it isn't linked.

USB mode

Both is controller + keyboard; MIDI and Keyboard use one only.

Recording timer

Pre-roll (0–10 s) the timer starts at when you press Record.

With the Bluetooth link the timer is the MixPre's own record time. The unit counts its pre-roll too but doesn't report it, so set this to the same number as on the MixPre.

Shortcut labels

Keyboard tools

Type text on the MixPre (track names, metadata) and two risky shortcuts.

Tips

  • Tap Rewind or Forward to skip, hold to scrub.
  • Faders jump to your finger; lit S/M/R buttons show the MixPre's state.
  • Keyboard mode can't read the MixPre back, so S/M/R lights are a guess and faders need MIDI.
  • Assign U1–U5 on the MixPre: Menu › ★ Shortcuts › Control Surface.
